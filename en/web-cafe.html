FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does a website for a specialty coffee shop take?

A first version can be ready in 7 to 14 days, depending on the content, photos, menu, and number of sections.

Can the website help me appear on Google?

Yes. The structure is prepared with local SEO: titles, content, speed, hierarchy, and geographic focus in the Balearic Islands.

Do I need professional photos?

Not necessarily, but they help a lot. If you do not have them yet, we can start with a clean visual direction and improve the imagery later.

Can I show my menu online?

Yes. We can include your menu, opening hours, location, WhatsApp, bookings, featured products, and social links.
